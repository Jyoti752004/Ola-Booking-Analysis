# Ola-Booking-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Ola bookings analysis using Excel, SQL, and Power BI. The objective was to perform exploratory data analysis, and build an interactive dashboard to analyze and visualize ride booking data to uncover key business insights. It helps understand customer behavior, driver performance, and operational efficiency through interactive Power BI visuals.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preprocessing)
* SQL (Data Analysis & Queries)
* Power BI (Dashboard Development)

* Total Records: 103024

## Key Insights

* Successful bookings form the majority of rides, showing strong operational efficiency.
* Sedans and SUVs tend to cover longer average ride distances compared to Autos.
* Customer‑initiated cancellations highlight reliability and satisfaction challenges.
* A small group of top customers contribute significantly to overall bookings.
* Driver cancellations due to personal and car‑related issues reveal operational bottlenecks.
* Prime Sedan rides show wide variation in driver ratings, indicating inconsistent service quality.
* UPI emerges as a popular payment method, reflecting digital adoption trends.
* Customer ratings vary across vehicle types, with premium categories generally scoring higher.
* Revenue insights show that successfully completed rides drive the bulk of booking value.
* Incomplete rides, with reasons logged, highlight areas for service improvement.
